Personal Finance Advisor Bot
Track income, manage expenses and receive personalized financial insights.
FinanceIndividual

The Personal Finance Advisor Bot is an AI-powered financial planning assistant developed to help individuals take control of their personal finances with clarity and confidence.

The system enables users to record monthly income, log daily and weekly expenses by category, receive personalized budget plans, and track saving progress within a centralized digital environment.

By integrating modern technologies with AI-driven financial analysis, the platform provides accurate budget guidance, intelligent spending insights, and structured monthly financial reporting.

Scenarios

Scenario 1: A salaried professional logs monthly income and daily expenses across rent, food, transport, and entertainment. The system identifies overspending areas and generates an actionable budget plan.

Scenario 2: A college student uses the advisor to manage a limited monthly allowance across essential and discretionary spending categories and receives recommendations for reducing unnecessary expenses.

Scenario 3: A freelancer with variable monthly income tracks earnings from multiple clients and project-related expenses. The system adapts recommendations based on income patterns.

Scenario 4: A household manager monitors shared family income and expenses across groceries, utilities, education, and healthcare and receives a consolidated monthly report.
